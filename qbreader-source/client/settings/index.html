Import QBReader stats

Bring your personal reading history into QBSS. Select the tossup and bonus CSV exports from your QBReader account; you can import either one or both. Reimporting the same file will not duplicate its records.

View Stats

Importing adds to your existing QBSS history. To move to a clean profile, keep both original CSVs and import them after setup.

Question database setup

Choose an extracted QBReader backup folder and QBSS will check its files, build the searchable database, and restart with it. Your stars and practice history stay separate.

Get a complete backup from QBReader’s public backups page. Keep all extracted files together. The database is processed locally and is not sent to QBSS or shared.
