Privacy in QBSS

QBSS runs on your computer and does not use a QBSS account or cloud sync service.

Stored on your computer

Your imported question database, starred questions, saved packets, and practice history are stored in the local QBSS data folder. Reader preferences are stored in the app's local browser storage. The backup importer processes the files you select on your computer.

Wikipedia

When you search or open an article, QBSS sends the search terms or article title to English Wikipedia. Wikimedia receives the network request and may process it under its privacy policy. Article text and resized images may be cached on your computer for offline reading.

Anki

If you use Add to Anki, the card fields you submit are sent to AnkiConnect on your own computer so Anki can create the note. QBSS does not send those fields to a QBSS server.

External links and data sharing

External links open in your browser and are governed by the destination site's policies. QBSS does not upload your local question database, stats CSVs, stars, or practice history. Anyone who can access files on your computer may be able to access the local QBSS data folder, so protect it and keep backups private.
