About QBSS

Quiz Bowl Study Suite (QBSS) is an independent, local-first study app adapted from the QBReader website project. QBSS is not the official QBReader website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by QBReader.

The original QBReader source was created by Geoffrey Wu, with contributions from the QBReader contributors. QBSS retains the original MIT license in its source folder.

QBSS adds local question database support, personal study history, an in-app Wikipedia reader, theme profiles, and an AnkiConnect bridge. Question and packet content is separate from the QBReader software license.
