Tips & Instructions

  • Open Study to choose your question source and practice mode.
  • Use the bottom toolbar to open Settings, the local question database, Wikipedia, or Add to Anki without losing your place.
  • Star questions in the reader, then find or play them from Library → Starred Questions.
  • Use Settings to import your QBReader tossup and bonus stats CSV files or a Base16 theme.
  • Read database setup for help downloading and importing a QBReader backup.

Your practice history and imported packets are stored locally. Keep the QBSS data folder private and back it up if you want to preserve it.
